## App name (≤30)
Vezoria – group travel

## Short description (≤80)
Plan, live and remember your trips. Free, no ads, works offline, encrypted.

## Full description (≤4000)
Vezoria keeps your whole trip in one place: the itinerary, bookings, budget and memories. It's free, has no ads, needs no account, and works even without a connection.

Plan
• Day-by-day itinerary: add or remove days, change dates
• Bookings: journeys, hotels, visits and more
• Import a booking from its PDF or confirmation email: it's read on your phone, the type is recognized, and if there's a duplicate on the same dates you choose to keep or replace it
• "What to see": places around each stop, plus your own places
• Budget: who pays what, payment schedule
• Wishes, ideas and a checklist

On the trip
• Today's sheet at a glance
• Geolocated steps
• Expenses with a photo of the receipt
• Route tracking while the app is open
• Memories with photos

Afterwards
• Your trips on a 3D globe
• Countries visited and stats
• ZIP backup
• Photo album as a PDF

Summary
• Who owes whom, refunds
• Balance per person and receipts

Travel together
Share a trip with your partner or friends using a code. Everyone has their own color and every edit is signed, so you always know who changed what.

Your data stays yours
• No account, no ads
• Everything works offline
• Optional sync, end-to-end encrypted
• Import your trips from a data export (ZIP)

Simple or complete, your choice
Hide the tabs and blocks you don't use to keep the app light. Available in 7 languages.

Have a great trip!
