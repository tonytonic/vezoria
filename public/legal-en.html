4. Nature and purpose of the app

The App is a free shared travel journal, designed and made available on a personal, non-commercial basis, open to everyone, without an account. It lets you plan a trip together: day-by-day itinerary, bookings, budget and expense sharing, memories and photos.

The App is provided free of charge. It is not sold, has no subscription and contains no advertising. The web version offers an optional donation link (Buy Me a Coffee) to help pay for the server; no feature is reserved for donors.

Always check the price and conditions with the provider before booking.

The “who owes what” calculation is an aid for sharing costs among friends and family; it has no contractual value.
