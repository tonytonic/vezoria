4. Nature and purpose of the app

The App is a free shared travel journal, published as part of a professional activity (sole proprietorship, see section 1), open to everyone, usable without an account on the phone (an account with an email address is required to put your journal online, see section 7). It lets you plan a trip together: day-by-day itinerary, bookings, budget and expense sharing, memories and photos. It also offers features built on the user’s phone: 3D flyover of the trip, shareable recap, badges and statistics, expense analysis, packing suggestions, and import of one’s own trips from another service.

All features of the App are provided free of charge to the user. Optional plans (Plus and Max, see section 8) will only add online space for photos. The App contains no advertising banners. It is funded by partner links: when a user books with certain partners (tours, transfers, car rental, eSIM…) through a link marked “partner”, the publisher may receive a commission, at no extra cost to the user. These links are always identified as such.

Access and acceptance. The App is used without an account on the phone; putting a notebook online requires an account (see below). Using it means accepting these terms and the privacy policy, which are presented and accepted the first time the App is opened.

Online notebook account (required). To put their notebook online (sync, shared trips, followers), users create an account: an e-mail address they verify, linked to their admin code. Without an account, the notebook stays only on the phone. Invited companions and followers do not need an account to join or follow a trip. A new device can then open the notebook only once approved, with a code sent to that address or from an already approved device. Users undertake to give an address they own, to keep their admin code confidential and to approve only their own devices; they remain responsible for what is done from the devices they have approved. They can change the address at any time or delete the account (☁️ Cloud → 👤 My account), which also erases their notebook from the server. The publisher never asks for the admin code.

User content. Each user is solely responsible for the texts, photos and comments they add, share with their companions or publish for followers. They undertake to publish only lawful content, to respect the privacy and image rights of the people photographed (ask for their consent before publishing their photo) and not to publish unlawful, pornographic or sexual, violent, insulting or hateful content, content harming a minor or infringing the rights of others.

Trips shared with others. By adding a text, photo or comment to a shared trip, each member allows the other members of that trip to view it, keep it in their copy of the trip and export it for their personal, private use. Republishing it elsewhere (in another trip, for followers, on a social network or any other medium) requires the consent of its author and of the people recognisable in the photo. The trip administrator organises the trip: they can remove a member and hand the administration over to another member; this role gives them no rights over the other members’ texts and photos. A member who leaves the trip keeps their copy; a member who is removed keeps a read-only copy of what their device contained. In both cases, for their personal use. Everyone can delete their own content; a disagreement between members is settled between them, and the publisher only acts upon a report of unlawful content (see below).

No monitoring of content. As content is encrypted on the user’s device, the publisher has no access to it and no obligation to monitor it. They only act upon a report, and may then remove or block a notebook and, where required by law, pass the information they hold to the competent authorities.

Sharing and followers. Anyone who receives a trip’s code or link (or its follower code) can read its content. It is up to the user to pass them only to the people of their choice. Followers can leave ❤️ and comments, under their own responsibility; the author of a trip can stop publishing at any time. A follower can follow a trip directly in their browser, without installing the app; their first name is optional (otherwise they appear as “A loved one”).

Import from another service. Users may import their own data into the App, for example the data file another travel journal app sends them on request. They warrant that this data is theirs or that they have the right to use it. The file is read on their phone and is not sent to the publisher. The App is independent of these services, is not affiliated with them and does not use their servers.

Recap, 3D flyover, badges and statistics. Images, videos, badges, percentages and analyses are built on the phone from the journal’s data. Packing suggestions. The ideas offered (based on forecast weather or last year’s records and on planned activities) are indicative only: they do not replace official requirements and advice (documents, health, safety).

Location. Trip tracking and sharing the route with followers are off by default. Each traveller turns them on themselves and can stop at any time.

Online space. Online space for photos is limited (about 1,500 photos for free, more with a plan: see section 8). Beyond that, new photo uploads are refused, without deleting anything; new photos stay on your phone. The service may change, be temporarily interrupted or discontinued; in the latter case, the publisher will try to give advance notice on this page so that everyone can export their trips.

Blocking. The author of a published trip can delete a comment, block a person (their comments and ❤️ are hidden for all followers) and create a new follower link to remove everyone’s access. Every user can hide what a person posts and unfollow a trip.

Reporting, suspension and deletion: how it works.

  1. Reporting. Unlawful content can be reported with the app’s “🚩 Report” button (⋯ menu of a post or comment) or to the contact address below, preferably with the code or link of the trip concerned: without it, the publisher cannot identify the notebook. The publisher does not disclose who made a report, unless required by law.
  2. Review. As data is encrypted, the publisher does not read it: they rely on the information sent with the report (description, screenshots) and handle each report as soon as possible.
  3. Suspension (reversible). As a precaution, the publisher may suspend the reported notebook or trip. Its members can no longer sync it or upload photos to it; when syncing, the app shows “This notebook is suspended following a report”, with the address to contest. Nothing is deleted and what everyone already has on their phone remains available.
  4. Contesting. The owner or a member can contest by writing to the contact address, stating the trip concerned and their explanation. If the report is unfounded or abusive, the notebook is restored: syncing resumes normally, with no loss.
  5. Permanent deletion. This only happens if the unlawful nature is established, or at the request of the competent authorities, to whom the publisher passes, where required by law, the information they hold. The notebook’s entries, photos, notification subscriptions and scheduled reminders are then deleted from the server, and the notebook stays blocked. Copies on phones are not affected: the publisher has no access to them.

Scope of a measure. A suspension or deletion only targets the reported notebook or trip, identified by its code. It never affects its members’ other notebooks or trips, nor those of other users. A shared trip has a single code common to all its members, so the measure applies to all of them. A publication for followers has its own code: it can be suspended without affecting the companions’ trip, and vice versa.

Deletion by the user. Anyone can delete their notebook or a shared trip from the server at any time in the app (☁️ Cloud, or Settings → “Delete my data from the server”). What is on their phone stays on their phone. For a shared trip, deletion applies to everyone who has its code: their phones keep what they already have, and the app offers to put it back online. When leaving the cloud, users can also cut their followers’ access to their published trips, including finished ones.

A false report made with intent to harm may engage its author’s liability.

8. Plus and Max subscription plans

Coming soon. The Plus and Max plans have been announced but are not open yet. Until they open, no payment can be requested. Their prices will be shown in the App, all taxes included, before any purchase, and this section will be completed at that time.

8.1 What the plans include. All features of the App remain free. A plan only adds online space for the photos of the notebook, shared trips included (photos from a trip’s companions count in the notebook of the person who created it, so they benefit from the plan too).

  • Free: about 1,500 photos (700 MB).
  • Plus: about 10,000 photos (4.8 GB).
  • Max: about 100,000 photos (50 GB), under fair use: the space is for the notebook’s travel photos, not for general backup; 50 GB is the technical ceiling.

The number of photos is indicative: space is measured by the size of the encrypted files stored on the server. In offline mode (no cloud), there is no limit.

8.2 Purchase and payment. Only the notebook’s owner (admin code) can subscribe. Payment goes through a reseller, which sells the subscription in its own name, collects the payment, issues the invoice and applies VAT:

  • in the Android app installed from Google Play: Google Play (Google Ireland Ltd), under Google Play’s terms;
  • on other devices (browser, iPhone, computer): a payment provider acting as merchant of record, named on the payment page.

The publisher never receives or stores bank details. The subscription is tied to the notebook, not to the device: it applies on every device where the notebook is open.

8.3 Term, renewal and cancellation. Subscriptions are monthly or yearly. They renew automatically for the same term until cancelled. You can cancel at any time, free of charge, in a few taps: in Google Play (Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions), or in the App (☁️ Cloud → “Manage my subscription”). Cancellation takes effect at the end of the period already paid, until which the space remains available. Before a yearly subscription renews, you are reminded, no earlier than 3 months and no later than 1 month before the renewal date, that you may choose not to renew.

8.4 Right of withdrawal. Consumers have 14 days from purchase to withdraw, without giving a reason, through the reseller or by writing to the contact address. As the space is available from purchase, at the consumer’s express request, the price paid is refunded minus an amount proportional to the period already elapsed.

8.5 Price changes. Any price change is announced at least 30 days before it applies. It only applies from the next renewal, and you may cancel before then.

8.6 When a subscription ends. Nothing is deleted. All photos stay online and viewable, by you as well as your companions and followers. Only new photo uploads stop while the space used exceeds the free space (about 1,500 photos): new photos then stay on the phone. Everything else keeps working: creating trips, sharing, comments, ❤️. You can get back under the free space by removing photos from the cloud, or subscribe again.

8.7 Keeping and deleting the notebook. Only you delete your notebook (☁️ Cloud, or Settings → “Delete my data from the server”). There are only two exceptions:

  • a notebook with no activity at all (no opening, no sync) for 24 months is deleted from the server. This never happens while a subscription is active;
  • removal of unlawful content, under the conditions of section 7.

In both cases, what is stored on phones is not affected. The ZIP backup lets you keep a full copy, photos included.

Photos stay on the user's phone: the App never removes them on its own. The online copy is used for sharing and for opening the journal on other devices; it is stored in a lighter version and does not replace a backup of the original photos (phone gallery, ZIP backup).

8.8 Gift codes. A gift code gives extra space for a stated period. It cannot be refunded or exchanged for money. It does not replace an active paid subscription that already gives as much space.

8.9 Guarantees and end of service. Paid plans benefit from the statutory conformity guarantee for digital services (articles L. 224-25-12 et seq. of the French Consumer Code). The limitations of liability in section 6 do not apply to these statutory guarantees. Should the publisher permanently close the online photo service, it would give at least 3 months’ notice and refund the unused part of the subscription.
